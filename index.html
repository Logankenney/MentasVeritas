What we get asked first.

Are we too small for this?

No. We built the working session for exactly that. The best AI projects are often small, specific and cheap to prove.

Which AI vendors do you use?

Whichever fits your data, budget and risk. We put the choice, and the reasons, in writing.

What happens to our data?

We keep it where it belongs, under no-training terms, and we delete working copies and confirm it in writing when we are done.

Do you work with companies outside your country?

Yes. We work remotely with clients wherever they are, and we review against the markets you actually serve.

Who owns what we build?

You do, on payment. We license anything of ours inside it to you permanently.
